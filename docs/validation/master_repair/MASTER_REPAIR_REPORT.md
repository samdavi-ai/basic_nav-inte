# VIRA master simulation repair campaign

**DO NOT INTEGRATE — ACCEPTANCE INCOMPLETE.** The deterministic Gazebo runs demonstrate unrecovered traversable routes and a synchronized insertion with negative padded-footprint clearance. The accepted launcher and mission components were retained as the controls. No physical validation was performed.

## Results and root causes

- **Geometry.** Independent configuration-space analysis reproduced the original patrol overlap between samples. A high-rate repeat of the revised study staging route found overlap at actual native Gazebo poses. The doorway turn can start with inadequate room despite satisfying the waypoint tolerance. See [geometry verification](GEOMETRY_VALIDATION.md).
- **Front insertion.** The versioned guard and accepted NavFn tree produced four replacement plans with positive frozen-scene margins. Repeated local braking or route interventions nevertheless consumed the waypoint retry budget. In the independent clearance-planner variant a rollout veto preceded rest, fresh marking and a replacement. The subsequent controller commands were RotationShim alignment commands. Translating DWB rollouts stopped, the release conditions failed, and the bounded recovery expired. An available global centerline is insufficient for this controller/guard combination. [Versioned guard](front/metrics.json) · [Clearance NavFn](front_v2/metrics.json)
- **Insertion during planning.** Planning activity and post-plan freshness holds reduced the measured overlap in one repeat, but an overlap was already present by the first scene confirmation. This experiment cannot establish a causal performance improvement across different timings. A controller veto issued after observation cannot prevent overlap that has already occurred. Neither the first risk notification nor a plan publication time establishes when the new collision geometry first became observable. [Insertion measurements](fenced_4/insertion_geometry.json)
- **Near goal.** The old barrier marker could persist after a cancelled second planner invocation. The first master trial inserted while the robot was still far from the fixture. Its action interval check failed. The revised fixture records exit on cancellation and verifies confirmation against the actual second invocation. A correctly synchronized near-goal instance still failed navigation. [Rejected timing](near/metrics.json) · [Verified repeat](fenced_9/metrics.json)
- **Crossings and blockages.** One moving crossing and the temporary blockage completed with matched terminal events and positive interpolated clearance. Gazebo confirmed actual removal in the temporary case. The mirrored actor now unsubscribes before shutdown and reached its endpoint, but this candidate failed navigation. The permanently blocked hallway terminated with correlated failure and a positive independent intersample bound. [Per-case results](DETERMINISTIC_RESULTS.md)
- **Static regressions.** Initial versioned-candidate corridor, open-study and patrol runs failed. Several reached PATH_VALIDATION with no release before the deadline. The planner copied the costmap and excluded centers that could never admit the padded footprint. This did not resolve the front, original-corridor or study failures. A separate turning-clearance patrol reached the kitchen staging pose but could not plan to the requested kitchen goal. Its final history undercounted progress: the launch log records dispatch of waypoint two while the failure history reports zero completed. The current coordinator subtracts one from the zero-based current index on failure.
- **E-stop.** All eight probes latched, observed zero, rejected a mission and passed explicit-reset motion checks. The private coordinator now records a correlated MISSION_CANCELLED before client timeout while keeping E-stop latched. Largest measured displacement was 0.0824 m, largest final-second component 0.0197, longest time to observe zero 3.74 ms. These are finite observation windows. See the [interruption matrix](ESTOP_INTERRUPTION_MATRIX.md).
- **Idle and ownership.** The three command streams were exactly zero after the completed doorway patrol and after private patrol failure. Restarting the coordinator and then Nav2 in separate owned process groups passed the subsequent three-second idle windows. A follow-on moving E-stop smoke test could not dispatch because the restarted coordinator reported uninitialized AMCL covariance. The gate rejected the request. The initial split-launch fixture omitted the terrain process and remained in health check; it was retained and corrected before retry. See [integration audit](INTEGRATION_SAFETY_AUDIT.md).
